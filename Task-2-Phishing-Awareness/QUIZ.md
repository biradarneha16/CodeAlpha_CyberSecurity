# Phishing Awareness Quiz

1. Account will be disabled in 10 minutes unless you use a link. First action? A) Click B) Pause and verify through the normal official service C) Forward D) Reply with password

2. Why inspect a link destination? A) Links are always malicious B) Visible text can differ from actual destination C) HTTPS never works D) Browsers cannot protect users

3. Authority-based social engineering? A) Message pretending to be your manager and requesting an unusual action B) Normal software update C) Scheduled meeting D) Reminder

4. Is HTTPS alone proof of legitimacy? A) Yes B) No C) Mobile only D) Banking only

5. Safest verification of unexpected payment request? A) Suspicious message contact B) Pay immediately C) Previously known trusted channel D) Post publicly

6. What reduces damage after password exposure? A) Reuse B) Unique passwords plus MFA C) Share D) Disable updates

7. After interacting with suspicious content? A) Hide B) Ignore C) Report promptly and follow incident response D) Download more files

8. Module sequence? A) Click→Guess→Reply→Delete B) Stop→Check→Verify→Report C) Open→Forward→Share→Trust D) Reply→Pay→Reset→Ignore

## Answer key
1-B, 2-B, 3-A, 4-B, 5-C, 6-B, 7-C, 8-B.
